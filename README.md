# CRT Student Organization Participation Tracker

A web system for **College for Research and Technology (CRT)** that helps student organizations run in one place instead of spreadsheets and paper logs.

Administrators, advisers, and students use the same platform to manage organizations, join events, assign and finish tasks, and keep attendance accurate.

---

## What problem it solves

Student organizations used to keep records in different files and notebooks. That made it hard to know who was a member, who attended, and who finished their work.

This system keeps **organizations, members, events, tasks, and attendance** together. Everyone sees the same up-to-date record.

That means:

- **Clearer management** — advisers and administrators handle organizations, memberships, and events from one dashboard
- **Better participation** — students see their tasks, deadlines, and attendance, and get reminders before something is missed
- **Honest reports** — participation reports come from real attendance and task records, not guesswork

---

## Who uses it

There are three portals. Each person signs in to the one that matches their role. Accounts are created by an administrator.

### Administrator

Runs the school-wide setup:

- Manage students and advisers
- Manage organizations and memberships
- Set up events and registrations
- Watch activity and generate reports

### Adviser

Runs the day-to-day life of an organization:

- Manage the organizations they advise
- Manage the students in those organizations
- Assign tasks with schedules and deadlines
- Record attendance (Present, Absent, or Excused)
- Follow task completion
- Generate participation reports

### Student

Takes part and keeps track of their own record:

- See organization memberships
- Browse and register for events
- View assigned tasks and deadlines
- Track task completion
- Check personal attendance
- Receive email notifications when something changes

---

## Main features

Two features sit at the center of the system:

1. **Task assignment and monitoring** — advisers assign work with a schedule and deadline, then follow completion as it happens
2. **Attendance tracking** — Present, Absent, or Excused is recorded, and a clean history is kept

Around those sit the rest of the cycle:

- Organization management (organizations, officers, advisers)
- Event management and online registration
- Student membership (add, approve, and review members)
- Automatic email reminders so deadlines are not missed

---

## How the system is used

1. **Administrators set things up** — students, advisers, organizations, events, and registrations
2. **Advisers run the organization** — they assign tasks and record attendance
3. **Students take part** — they join events, complete tasks, and check their own records

### How tasks and attendance work together

1. The adviser assigns a task, picks the students, and sets a schedule and deadline
2. The adviser records attendance: Present, Absent, or Excused
3. The system updates the related task automatically
4. The student portal shows the new status and attendance right away
5. An email is sent when a record changes

| If attendance is… | The task becomes… | What that means |
|-------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Present | Pending | Waiting until the adviser marks it Complete |
| Absent | Not Complete | The work was not done |
| Excused | Incomplete | It can still be finished later |

Other statuses you will see:

- **Pending** — assigned, waiting to be evaluated
- **Complete** — the student finished the task
- **Incomplete** — started or excused; can still be finished
- **Not Complete** — not done (for example, when absent)

## Common questions

**How do I get an account?**  
An administrator creates and manages accounts. When yours is ready, sign in through the portal that matches your role (administrator, adviser, or student).

**Where can I see my tasks and deadlines?**  
In the Student Portal. Tasks assigned by your adviser appear with their schedule, deadline, and current status.

**What happens to a task if I am absent or excused?**  
When your adviser marks you Absent or Excused, the system updates the related task automatically. You will see the new status in your portal.

**How can I check my attendance?**  
Your attendance history is in the Student Portal. It updates as soon as your adviser records it.
